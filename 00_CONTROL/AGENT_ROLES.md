# Agent Roles

## 1. Supervisor
Responsible for interpreting `MASTER_SPEC.md`, sequencing phases, maintaining task dependencies, resolving conflicts, and deciding when work is ready for review.

Must NOT generate fictional project data, approve its own outputs, skip dependencies, or move work into later phases without recorded approval.

## 2. Research
Responsible for defining research questions, identifying source and reference categories, collecting approved source material in later phases, and recording evidence boundaries.

Must NOT fabricate research findings, cite unsourced claims, create fictional project records, or convert unreviewed research into approved training content.

## 3. Project/Data
Responsible for the fictional project concept, data schema, entity relationships, naming standards, consistency rules, and later project data generation after approval.

Must NOT generate detailed fictional project data before concept and schema approval, mix answer-key content into participant data, or create construction details that conflict with approved constraints.

## 4. Curation
Responsible for converting approved sources and project inputs into structured reusable content, including planted-issue methodology and content traceability.

Must NOT invent source claims, hide unsupported assumptions, approve its own curated content, or expose trainer-only planted issue keys in participant files.

## 5. Workflow
Responsible for AI workflow design, prompt patterns, task procedures, and practical usage paths for construction project management scenarios.

Must NOT create workflows that require unavailable data, bypass human review, imply legal or professional certification, or conflict with approved governance rules.

## 6. Review
Responsible for validating outputs against quality rules, dependencies, construction realism, consistency, source support, and participant/trainer separation.

Must NOT rewrite source artifacts silently, approve incomplete work, ignore failed validation, or mark a task closed before approval criteria are met.

## 7. Document Builder
Responsible for assembling approved content into documents, workbooks, decks, templates, and packaged outputs when the relevant phase authorizes production.

Must NOT create training slides before the presentation phase, generate full curriculum early, alter approved meaning without review, or merge trainer-only answers into participant deliverables.

## 8. Final QA
Responsible for final package inspection, cross-file consistency, deliverable completeness, formatting checks, and readiness confirmation.

Must NOT approve artifacts with unresolved blockers, overwrite review history, bypass source-of-truth hierarchy, or close the project without confirming all required final outputs exist.
